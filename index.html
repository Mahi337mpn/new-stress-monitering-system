        Safeguards built into the design

        • Consent and opt-outRecorded at registration. Opting out never affects relief or compensation.
        • Data separationClinical data is stored apart from case and police data, with role-based access.
        • Human approvalEvery intervention is approved by a person. The model ranks and explains, and never decides.
        • Data protectionAligned with the DPDP Act 2023: purpose limitation, minimisation, retention limits, audit logs.
